# Campus Student Services, Library & IT Handbook

## 1. Central University Library
- **Operating Hours:** Monday–Saturday: **8:00 AM to 11:00 PM**; Sunday: **10:00 AM to 8:00 PM**. During Final Examination Weeks (Weeks 14–16), the Reading Hall operates **24/7**.
- **Borrowing Privileges:** Undergraduate students may borrow up to **6 books for 14 days**; postgraduate students may borrow up to **10 books for 21 days**. Overdue fines are **INR 5 per book per day**.
- **Digital Resources:** Off-campus access to IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, SpringerLink, and arXiv tools is available via University SSO credentials.

## 2. Computing Labs & GPU Cluster Access
- The **AI & High-Performance Computing Lab (Block C, Room 105)** provides workstations with NVIDIA RTX GPUs for students enrolled in CS302, CS401, or approved UROP research teams.
- Students can reserve GPU compute slots (up to **12 hours per week**) via the Campus Compute Scheduler portal using their university email.

## 3. Student Counseling & Wellness Center
- Free, strictly confidential mental health counseling, exam stress workshops, and peer support are available at the **Wellness Center (Student Activity Block, 2nd Floor)**.
- Walk-in hours: Monday–Friday, **9:30 AM to 5:30 PM**. 24/7 Campus Wellness Helpline: **1800-555-0199**.

## 4. Academic Advising & Career Placement Cell
- Every student is assigned a **Faculty Advisor** upon admission to assist with course selection, credit overloads, internships, and academic appeals.
- **Summer Internship Policy:** Students completing Semester 6 must complete a mandatory **6-to-8 week industry or research internship** (worth 2 credits) and submit an Internship Completion Report by August 20.
- The **Training & Placement Cell** conducts resume reviews, mock technical interviews, and campus recruitment drives starting in **August** of the final year.

## 5. Hostel & IT Support
- Campus Wi-Fi (`Campus-EduRoam`) credentials are linked to the student registration number. Password resets and portal issues are handled by the **IT Helpdesk** (`helpdesk@university.edu` or Block A, Room 102) with a standard turnaround time of **24 hours**.
- Hostel curfew for first-year students is **10:30 PM** on weekdays and **11:30 PM** on weekends; late-entry passes for library or lab work require digital approval from the Lab Supervisor or Warden.
