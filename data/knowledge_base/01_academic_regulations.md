# University Academic Regulations & Attendance Policy (2026–2027)

## 1. Credit System and Degree Requirements
- The undergraduate Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) / Bachelor of Science (B.S.) degree requires completion of **160 credits** over 8 semesters.
- Students normally register for **18 to 22 credits per semester**. Taking more than 22 credits (up to a maximum of 26 credits) requires a minimum CGPA of **8.0 / 10.0** and formal approval from the Faculty Advisor and Head of Department (HoD).
- A minimum cumulative grade point average (**CGPA**) of **5.0** and zero pending core backlogs are mandatory for graduation.

## 2. Attendance Policy
- Students must maintain a minimum of **75% attendance** in every registered theory course and laboratory course independently to be eligible for End-Semester Examinations.
- Students with attendance between **65% and 74.9%** may apply for **Medical or Co-Curricular Condonation** within 5 working days of the final lecture date by submitting verified medical certificates from the Campus Health Center or an official sports/conference participation letter approved by the Dean of Student Affairs.
- A condonation fee of **INR 1,500 per course** applies for non-medical approved co-curricular condonation; verified hospitalization medical condonation has no fee.
- Students with **below 65% attendance** are automatically placed in the **Detained (DT)** category for that course and must re-register for the course in a subsequent semester or Summer Term.

## 3. Course Add/Drop and Withdrawal
- **Add/Drop Window:** Students may add or drop courses without academic penalty during the first **10 working days** of the semester via the Student Academic Portal.
- **Course Withdrawal (W Grade):** After the Add/Drop deadline and up to **Week 8** of the semester, students may withdraw from a course with advisor approval. A `W` grade appears on the transcript but does not impact SGPA or CGPA.
- Dropping below **14 credits** in a regular semester requires special permission from the Academic Senate Committee.

## 4. Academic Integrity and AI Usage Policy
- Plagiarism, unauthorized collaboration, contract cheating, and fabrication of experimental data constitute severe academic misconduct.
- **Generative AI Policy:** Use of LLMs (e.g., Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude) is **permitted for brainstorming, concept clarification, and debugging**, provided students include an **AI Disclosure Statement** citing the tool and prompts used.
- Submitting AI-generated code, essays, or exam answers verbatim without attribution or where explicitly prohibited by the instructor results in an automatic **zero on the assignment** for a first offense and referral to the **Disciplinary Committee** (up to one-semester suspension) for a repeat offense.

## 5. Academic Probation and Honors
- Students whose SGPA falls below **5.0** in any semester are placed on **Academic Probation** and assigned mandatory weekly mentoring sessions.
- Students achieving an SGPA of **9.0 or above** with at least 18 credits and no failing or withdrawn grades are named to the **Dean's Merit List** for that semester.
