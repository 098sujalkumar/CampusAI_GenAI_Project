# Scholarships, Financial Aid & Research Grants Guide

## 1. Merit-Based Scholarships
- **Chancellor's Excellence Scholarship:** Awarded to the top **5% of students** in each department who maintain a CGPA of **9.2 or higher** with no disciplinary record or backlogs. Covers **100% of annual tuition fees**.
- **Dean's Academic Merit Award:** Awarded to students with a CGPA between **8.5 and 9.19**. Covers **50% of tuition fees** for the following academic year.
- Renewal requires maintaining the threshold CGPA at the end of every academic year and at least **80% overall attendance**.

## 2. Need-Based Financial Aid
- **Student Equity Grant:** Available to students with verified annual family income below **INR 6,00,000**. Provides **25% to 75% tuition fee waiver** depending on financial need tier.
- Required documents: Income certificate issued by a Tehsildar/Revenue Authority, previous year's bank statement, and Student Aid Application Form submitted by **September 15** each year.
- Students receiving need-based aid must maintain a minimum CGPA of **6.5** for continuation.

## 3. Undergraduate Research & Innovation Grants
- **Undergraduate Research Opportunity Program (UROP):** Provides up to **INR 40,000 per team** for hardware, cloud compute credits (GPU/API credits), and dataset acquisition for faculty-mentored AI/Engineering projects.
- **Conference Travel Grant:** Undergraduate and graduate students whose papers are accepted at peer-reviewed IEEE, ACM, NeurIPS, ICML, or ACL venues may apply for up to **INR 75,000** (international) or **INR 15,000** (domestic) toward registration and travel.

## 4. On-Campus Work-Study & Teaching Assistantships
- Senior undergraduates (Semester 5 and above) with a CGPA of **8.0+** and at least an **A grade** in the target course can apply to become **Undergraduate Teaching Assistants (UTAs)**.
- UTAs commit **6 to 8 hours per week** assisting in labs and office hours and receive a monthly stipend of **INR 8,000**.
- Applications open two weeks prior to each semester through the Office of Academic Affairs portal.
