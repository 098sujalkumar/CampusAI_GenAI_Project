# Course Syllabus: CS302 — Machine Learning Systems & Applied NLP

**Credits:** 4 (3 Lectures + 1 Lab) | **Prerequisites:** Data Structures & Algorithms, Statistics
**Instructor:** Dr. Vikram Deshmukh | **Office Hours:** Mondays & Wednesdays, 2:00 PM – 4:00 PM (Block B, Room 208)

## Unit 1: Supervised Learning & Model Generalization (Weeks 1–3)
- Bias-Variance Tradeoff, regularization (L1 Lasso for sparsity, L2 Ridge for weight decay), and cross-validation (k-fold, stratified).
- Linear and Logistic Regression, Support Vector Machines (kernel trick), Decision Trees, Random Forests, and Gradient Boosted Trees (XGBoost, LightGBM).
- Evaluation metrics for classification: Precision, Recall, F1-Score, ROC-AUC, and Confusion Matrix analysis for imbalanced datasets.

## Unit 2: Unsupervised Learning & Representation Learning (Weeks 4–6)
- K-Means clustering, DBSCAN, Hierarchical clustering, and Silhouette Score.
- Dimensionality reduction: Principal Component Analysis (PCA), t-SNE, and UMAP for visualizing high-dimensional embedding spaces.

## Unit 3: Neural Networks & Deep Learning Fundamentals (Weeks 7–9)
- Backpropagation, activation functions (ReLU, GELU, Softmax), vanishing/exploding gradients, Batch Normalization, and Dropout.
- Optimizers: SGD with Momentum, RMSProp, and AdamW.
- Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) for vision and Recurrent Neural Networks (LSTMs/GRUs) for sequential modeling.

## Unit 4: Classical & Neural Natural Language Processing (Weeks 10–12)
- Text preprocessing: tokenization, stemming, lemmatization, stop-word filtering, and n-grams.
- Bag-of-Words, TF-IDF (Term Frequency–Inverse Document Frequency), and BM25 ranking functions.
- Static word embeddings (Word2Vec Skip-Gram/CBOW, GloVe) vs. contextualized embeddings (ELMo, BERT).

## Unit 5: MLOps, Deployment & Monitoring (Weeks 13–14)
- Experiment tracking, dataset versioning, model serialization, and containerization with Docker.
- Detecting data drift and concept drift in production ML systems; latency vs. throughput trade-offs.
