# Examination, Continuous Assessment & Grading Handbook

## 1. Evaluation Breakdown
Unless otherwise specified in the course syllabus, every 4-credit theory course follows a **40% Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE)** and **60% End-Semester Examination (ESE)** structure:
- **Quizzes & In-Class Assessments:** 10%
- **Mid-Semester Examination (MSE):** 15% (conducted in Week 7, 90 minutes)
- **Assignments / Course Project / Lab Demos:** 15%
- **End-Semester Examination (ESE):** 60% (conducted in Weeks 15–16, 3 hours)

## 2. Relative & Absolute Grading Scale (10-Point Scale)
| Letter Grade | Grade Points | Score Range (Absolute Guideline) | Status |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **O (Outstanding)** | 10 | 90 – 100 | Pass |
| **A+ (Excellent)** | 9 | 80 – 89 | Pass |
| **A (Very Good)** | 8 | 70 – 79 | Pass |
| **B+ (Good)** | 7 | 60 – 69 | Pass |
| **B (Above Average)** | 6 | 50 – 59 | Pass |
| **C (Average)** | 5 | 45 – 49 | Pass |
| **P (Pass)** | 4 | 40 – 44 | Pass |
| **F (Fail)** | 0 | Below 40 | Fail |

- To pass a course, a student must obtain at least **40% overall** AND at least **35% in the End-Semester Examination** independently.

## 3. SGPA and CGPA Calculation
- **SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average)** = Sum of (Course Credits x Grade Points Earned) divided by Total Registered Credits in that semester.
- **CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average)** = Sum of (Course Credits x Grade Points Earned across all completed semesters) divided by Total Credits attempted (using the highest grade for repeated courses).

## 4. Re-Evaluation, Make-Up Exams, and Supplementary Exams
- **Answer Script Viewing:** Students can inspect evaluated ESE answer scripts within **3 working days** of result publication.
- **Re-Evaluation:** Requests for paper re-evaluation must be submitted online within **7 days** of result publication with a fee of **INR 1,000 per course**. If the score increases by **5% or more**, the re-evaluation fee is refunded in full.
- **Make-Up Mid-Semester Exam:** Granted only for documented medical emergencies or university-sponsored national competitions; application must be submitted within 48 hours of the missed exam.
- **Supplementary Examination:** Conducted during the Winter/Summer break for students who received an `F` grade (not `DT`). The maximum grade awarded in a supplementary exam is capped at **B+ (7 points)** unless cleared due to an approved medical absence during the regular ESE.

## 5. Examination Hall Rules
- Students must carry a valid **University Photo ID Card** and **Exam Hall Ticket**.
- Programmable calculators, smartwatches, and mobile phones are strictly prohibited inside the examination hall. Only non-programmable scientific calculators are allowed when authorized on the question paper.
- Late entry is permitted only up to **20 minutes** after the exam start time; students may not leave the hall during the first **60 minutes**.
